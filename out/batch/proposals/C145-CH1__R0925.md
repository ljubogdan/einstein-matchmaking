# Matching podcast episode content to ad intent in SoundSlot

**Company:** Mediavox a.d. [C145], Marketing tech, media and creative studios, 87 employees, Novi Sad.  
**Researcher:** Milan Petrović, Assistant Professor, Faculty of Technical Sciences, Department of Computing and Control Engineering [R0925].  
**Format:** Joint project  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Assistant Professor as principal investigator and project lead (academic) and Senior backend developer as company technical contact and integration lead (company)  

### The problem
- SoundSlot targets listeners using age bracket, city and device type, and average pre-roll click-through sits at 0.9 percent across the network. [C145-CH1]
- Advertisers will not renew above a certain spend level until a 2.5 percent CTR can be demonstrated, and the company does not have anyone who knows how to build a content-embedding or topic-classification layer. [C145-CH1]
- The company holds episode transcripts going back four years and campaign performance logs but has not connected them to ad-decision logic. [C145-CH1]

### Work packages
1. Can transformer-based sentence embeddings of Serbian podcast episode transcripts produce topic clusters that separate episode intent signals, measured against five annotators' consensus labels on 500 held-out transcript segments annotated during Phase 1?
2. Does an intent-matching layer trained on episode embeddings and campaign performance logs raise pre-roll click-through rate toward 2.5 percent, measured against the current 0.9 percent baseline in a controlled A/B split of SoundSlot inventory over a four-week window in Phase 3?
3. How well do script-unified embedding models handle Latin and Cyrillic variation in Serbian podcast transcripts, measured against cross-script retrieval accuracy on a held-out transcript sample?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 – Data preparation, annotation and baseline (months 1–2) | Company data analyst prepares four years of episode transcripts and campaign logs into a structured dataset; university research assistant recruits five annotators from the SRB-NLP community, manages labelling of 500 transcript segments, and trains a baseline topic-classification model. | A cleaned transcript-and-campaign dataset, a 500-segment annotated set with five-annotator consensus labels, and a baseline classification report delivered at M2. |
| Phase 2 – Embedding and intent-matching model (months 3–4) | University lead researcher builds content-embedding and topic-classification layer using transformer-based sentence encoders; company backend developer integrates the layer into SoundSlot staging environment. | An intent-matching prototype running in the SoundSlot staging environment, delivered at M4. |
| Phase 3 – A/B validation and final report (months 5–6) | Company data analyst sets up and reports a four-week A/B split of live SoundSlot inventory, assigning half to embedding-matched targeting and half to current demographic targeting; university lead researcher analyses results and writes the final report. | An A/B split report comparing click-through rates across the two targeting conditions on live inventory and a final project report, delivered at M6. |
