# Compostable lidding film that seals to existing rPET punnet tooling

**Company:** FreshPak Solutions [C036], Packaging, 60 employees, NTP Novi Sad.  
**Researcher:** Stefan Đorđević, Associate Professor, Faculty of Technology, Department of Materials Engineering [R0274].  
**Format:** Joint project  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Associate Professor, Department of Materials Engineering (academic) and Project Manager (part-time), FreshPak Solutions (company)

### The problem
- Two of the three biggest customers are own-label suppliers for retail chains where conventional plastic lidding films will not be accepted from 2028 onward. [C036-CH2]
- Preliminary bench tests with PLA-based films showed seal strength was about thirty percent below current spec, and one film showed visible clouding at 4 °C refrigerated storage. [C036-CH2]
- The company does not have the materials chemistry background to reformulate or identify better candidate polymers, and has a budget allocated for a two-year development programme. [C036-CH2]

### Work packages
1. Which PLA-based polymer systems or multilayer formulations can meet the existing seal-strength specification when bonded to rPET punnet tooling at current process temperatures and dwell times?
2. What barrier modification approaches yield an oxygen and CO₂ transmission profile adequate for soft-fruit storage while retaining EN 13432 compostability?
3. What formulation or coating strategy eliminates visible anti-fog failure under refrigerated storage conditions?
4. Which candidate formulation or shortlist of materials can accumulate sufficient disintegration and ecotoxicity data to support an EN 13432 certification submission by mid-2027?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 — Candidate screening (months 1–6) | Review polymer systems and modification approaches compatible with existing rPET tooling constraints; prepare small-scale trial films; assess seal strength and anti-fog performance against defined pass criteria. | Ranked shortlist of candidate formulations or materials delivered at milestone M6. |
| Phase 2 — Tooling compatibility testing (months 7–12) | Produce lead formulation films and test seal strength using in-house pull-testing equipment against the commercial rPET punnet tooling specification at current process temperatures and dwell times. | Lead formulation or formulations confirmed or refined against tooling specification at milestone M12. |
| Phase 3 — Full barrier and compostability screening (months 13–18) | Measure anti-fog performance, OTR and CO₂TR on lead formulations using in-house benchtop analysers; run EN 13432 compostability screening tests and compile results into a structured data package. | Complete anti-fog, OTR/CO₂TR and EN 13432 screening data package delivered at milestone M18. |
| Phase 4 — Final formulation and certification data package (months 19–24) | Optimise the selected formulation based on Phase 3 results; assemble all test data and supporting documentation into a format suitable for EN 13432 certification submission. | Final formulation with complete supporting data package ready for EN 13432 certification submission at milestone M24. |
