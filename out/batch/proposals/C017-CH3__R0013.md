# Lime dose recommendations matched to Vojvodina soil series

**Company:** TloCare d.o.o. [C017], Agri-tech (farm software, sensing, drones), 22 employees, Novi Sad.  
**Researcher:** Igor Marić, Assistant Professor, Faculty of Agriculture, Department of Plant and Environmental Protection [R0013].  
**Format:** Contract research  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Assistant Professor, Department of Plant and Environmental Protection (academic) and TloCare technical lead (software) (company)

### The problem
- The current recommendation gives lime quantities based on measured pH and a simple table taken from an older Vojvodina handbook. [C017-CH3]
- Sandy-loam soils in river lowlands and heavy clay soils on the loess terrace respond differently to the same lime doses, and their buffer capacities differ drastically. [C017-CH3]
- The team does not have the agronomy depth to model buffer capacity properly for Vojvodina soil series. [C017-CH3]

### Work packages
1. How do SMP buffer pH and alternative methods compare against measured lime requirement across chernozem, hydromorphic, and alluvial soil series in Vojvodina when calibrated with texture and humus as covariates?
2. Which combination of pH, texture class, and humus content best predicts CaCO3 dose for each major Vojvodina soil series?
3. How does measurement uncertainty in pH, texture, and organic carbon inputs propagate into lime dose recommendation uncertainty across different soil types?
4. Do model predictions of CaCO3 dose align with outcomes recorded in the company multi-year parcel measurement data?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| M1–M3: Soil series classification and buffer capacity parameterisation | Select and analyse representative Vojvodina soil samples for buffer curves and lime requirement by texture class and humus content, drawing on the soil archive and parcel data, to address research questions 1 and 2. | Soil series classification and buffer capacity parameter set delivered. |
| M3–M5: Model construction and uncertainty analysis | Fit and cross-validate lime requirement models using pH, texture, and humus inputs across the classified soil series; run sensitivity analysis to quantify how input measurement uncertainty propagates to dose uncertainty, addressing research question 3. | Model equations with uncertainty bounds for each soil series delivered. |
| M5–M6: Portal integration and pilot testing | Implement the model in the TloCare portal so that pH, texture, and humus values entered per parcel return a CaCO3 dose recommendation adjusted by soil series. | Prototype liming recommendation model delivered and integrated into TloCare portal. |
| M6–M9: Field validation and final calibration | Compare model outputs against multi-year parcel measurement records; adjust calibration parameters as needed; prepare final model documentation, addressing research question 4. | Field validation completed and final model delivered ready for spring season. |
