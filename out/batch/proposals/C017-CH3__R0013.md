# Liming recommendations matched to Vojvodina soil type, not just pH

**Company:** TloCare d.o.o. [C017], Agri-tech (farm software, sensing, drones), 22 employees, Novi Sad.  
**Researcher:** Igor Marić, Assistant Professor, Faculty of Agriculture, Department of Plant and Environmental Protection [R0013].  
**Format:** Contract research  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Assistant Professor, Department of Plant and Environmental Protection (academic) and Product lead, TloCare d.o.o. (company)  

### The problem
- The current recommendation uses a pH reading and a table from an older Vojvodina handbook, without accounting for buffer capacity differences between soil types. [C017-CH3]
- Sandy-loam soils in floodplains and heavy clay soils on the loess terrace respond differently to the same lime dose, and clients have reported this for years. [C017-CH3]
- The team has agrochemistry skills but lacks the capacity to build a buffer capacity model applied to specific Vojvodina soil series. [C017-CH3]

### Work packages
1. Which combination of pH, texture, and humus inputs best predicts lime requirement across hydromorphic and chernozem soil series, measured against incubation lime requirement values from the 800-sample archive?
2. How accurately do SMP buffer equations calibrated per soil series predict CaCO3 dose, measured against observed pHKCl shift after known CaCO3 application rates from the Rimski Šančevi 12-year field experiment dataset?
3. Do predicted lime doses align with measured pH recovery after liming across light, medium, and heavy texture classes, measured against CaCO3 application and subsequent pH response pairs drawn from the Rimski Šančevi trial and any TloCare parcels where lime dose and follow-up pH are both recorded?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 — Months 1–3: Soil series classification and buffer equations | Researcher classifies Vojvodina soil series into modelling groups and derives SMP buffer capacity equations per group using the 800-sample archive and published calibration data. | Soil series classification scheme and buffer capacity equations document, delivered at M3. |
| Phase 2 — Months 4–6: Model calibration and validation | Researcher validates lime requirement model against observed pHKCl shift and CaCO3 application rates from the Rimski Šančevi 12-year field experiment; GIS specialist links TloCare PostGIS parcel layers where lime dose and follow-up pH are both recorded; error bounds are computed per texture class. | Validated lime requirement model with error bounds and a dataset of predicted vs. observed lime responses, delivered at M6. |

| Phase 4 — Month 9: Documentation and handover | Researcher writes final technical report; TloCare developers finalise API documentation; both sides review for confidential content within 30 days as agreed. | Final report, API documentation, and a publication-ready manuscript delivered at M9. |
