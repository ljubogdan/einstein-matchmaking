# Cleaning twelve years of legacy accounting exports without manual rework

**Company:** Petrović & Lukić IT Preduzetnik [C124], Custom software and IT services for EU clients, 4 employees, Zrenjanin.  
**Researcher:** Slađana Ranković, Senior Research Associate, Faculty of Sciences, Department of Mathematics and Informatics [R0181].  
**Format:** Consulting  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Senior Research Associate, Department of Mathematics and Informatics (academic) and Managing director (Dragan Petrović) (company)  

### The problem
- A fixed-price migration contract has reached 340 hours against a 200-hour quote with roughly three years of data still to clean. [C124-CH2]
- Every annual backup from 2008 to 2016 uses a slightly different field structure, and two years were exported from an intermediate system that added its own non-standard columns. [C124-CH2]
- The company has written about fifteen Python scripts for specific quirks, but each new batch of files tends to introduce at least one more, and no extra staff can be assigned. [C124-CH2]

### Work packages
1. Which schema variation patterns in the 2008–2016 exports can be detected and classified automatically, measured against a complete manual catalogue produced in month one?
2. Can a constraint-propagation matching pipeline resolve format variations across all twelve export years without per-batch manual edits, measured against technician review of 500 records per year?
3. Does the automated pipeline reach the same matching precision as manual matching on the actual accounting export samples, measured against a manually labelled reference set drawn from the same exports?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 — Audit and catalogue | Senior researcher reviews existing Python scripts and all available export samples with the junior developer; together they produce a structured catalogue of format variation types. | A format variation catalogue covering all identified schema patterns across the twelve export years, delivered as a shared document. |
| Month 2 — Pipeline prototype | Senior researcher designs the schema-matching and ETL pipeline architecture; junior developer implements it in Python against the catalogued variation types, using the researcher's benchmark datasets for calibration. | A runnable prototype pipeline that processes a representative subset of export years and a written design specification owned by the company. |
| Month 3 — Validation and integration | Junior developer runs the pipeline over remaining export years and manually labels 500 records drawn from a stratified sample of four representative years; senior researcher advises on failure cases; pipeline is integrated into the live migration workflow. | A validated pipeline integrated into the migration workflow, a test report comparing automated and manual matching precision on the 500-record reference set, and a decision on production readiness. |
