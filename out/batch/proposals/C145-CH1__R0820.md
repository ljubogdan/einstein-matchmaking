# Targeting podcast pre-roll ads by episode content to lift click-through rates

**Company:** Mediavox a.d. [C145], Marketing tech, media and creative studios, 87 employees, Novi Sad.  
**Researcher:** Branka Ivković, Full Professor, Faculty of Economics in Subotica, Department of Marketing [R0820].  
**Format:** Contract research  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Full Professor, Department of Marketing (principal investigator and delivery lead) (academic) and Head of Technology (milestone sign-off and technical liaison) (company)

### The problem
- Pre-roll click-through rate sits at 0.9 percent across the network, and advertisers will not renew above a certain spend level until the platform can demonstrate 2.5 percent CTR. [C145-CH1]
- Targeting currently relies on three variables — age bracket, city, and device type — drawn from registration data and IP lookup. [C145-CH1]
- The company holds episode transcripts and campaign logs but lacks internal expertise to build a content-embedding or topic-classification layer. [C145-CH1]

### Work packages
1. Which topic classification and content-embedding approaches best capture intent-relevant signals from podcast episode transcripts?
2. How does aligning pre-roll ad placement with episode-level content context affect click-through rate relative to the existing demographic-only targeting baseline?
3. At what level of contextual signal accuracy does the content-context matching layer produce a measurable shift in ad-decision outcomes?
4. How should the content-context matching layer be structured to support integration into the existing ad-decision logic for production deployment?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Data preparation and baseline establishment | Deliver the full transcript and campaign log dataset under a signed data access agreement, document current targeting variables, and establish a CTR baseline segmented by show genre and ad category to anchor all subsequent evaluation. | A structured, research-ready dataset and a documented performance baseline against which prototype improvements will be measured, enabling work on all research questions. |
| Classification model and embedding design | Design and compare topic classification approaches and content-embedding frameworks applicable to the transcript corpus; define accuracy threshold criteria and agree on the method that best captures episode-level intent signals. | A documented model specification covering the chosen topic classification and embedding approach, directly addressing research questions one and three. |
| Prototype build and historical testing | Build the content-context matching layer, connect it to the ad-decision logic with backend developer support, and test CTR predictions against historical campaign performance logs. | A working prototype evaluated on historical data, with results addressing research question two and demonstrating whether a path to the target CTR is achievable. |
| Evaluation and integration planning | Produce an evaluation report covering CTR uplift projections from the prototype and a set of recommendations for adapting the matching layer for production deployment within the existing ad server. | A delivered evaluation report with integration recommendations, addressing research question four and fulfilling the contracted prototype scope. |
