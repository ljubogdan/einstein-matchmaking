# Irrigation scheduling built into NjivaManager before the 2026 season

**Company:** Njivomer Agro d.o.o. [C022], Agri-tech (farm software, sensing, drones), 61 employees, Sombor.  
**Researcher:** Nenad Ilić, Associate Professor, Faculty of Agriculture, Department of Field and Vegetable Crops [R0662].  
**Format:** Joint project  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Associate Professor (principal investigator) (academic) and Lead developer / project coordinator (company)  

### The problem
- Njivomer lacks the in-house knowledge to implement FAO Penman-Monteith evapotranspiration correctly or to connect soil water-holding capacity and crop coefficients to its parcel database. [C022-CH3]
- Farm clients have requested irrigation scheduling advice in NjivaManager, and the company needs the calculation engine built and validated using real measured data before the 2026 irrigation season. [C022-CH3]

### Work packages
1. How accurately does a FAO Penman-Monteith daily water-balance engine estimate soil water depletion for maize, sunflower, and sugar beet when driven by Njivomer's three agrometeorological stations, measured against multi-depth probe readings and farmer-recorded irrigation volumes on at least two validation parcels across the 2025 irrigation season?
2. Which crop coefficient values, assigned by growth stage, minimise the difference between modelled and probe-measured soil water content across Vojvodina chernozem and sandy loam parcels in Njivomer's database, measured against observed ETc derived from the soil water balance and recorded irrigation volumes during the 2025 season?
3. How closely do soil water-holding capacity values derived from Njivomer's existing soil-type raster layers match field-measured values at the validation parcels, measured against direct laboratory analysis of samples taken at the start of the 2025 irrigation season?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 – Months 1–3 (Q4 2024): Engine prototype | University builds the FAO Penman-Monteith ET engine with crop coefficients for maize, sunflower, sugar beet, and soy; company GIS analyst aligns soil-type raster layers and supplies parcel database schema. | Runnable engine prototype processing daily weather inputs; crop coefficient lookup table for four crops at four growth stages. |

| Phase 4 – Months 13–18 (October 2025 – March 2026): Handover before 2026 season | University prepares documented calculation engine and developer handover materials incorporating validation findings; company developer reviews and signs off maintainability; both sides resolve corrections identified during validation. | Validated engine and full technical documentation delivered to Njivomer developers; decision on production deployment confirmed before the 2026 irrigation season. |
