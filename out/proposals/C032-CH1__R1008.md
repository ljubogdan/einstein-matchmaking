# Catching unusual OT network behaviour without labelled attack data

**Company:** SecureEdge Labs [C032], Cybersecurity, 19 employees, NTP Novi Sad.  
**Researcher:** Krisztina Kiss, Full Professor, Faculty of Technical Sciences, Department of Computing and Control Engineering [R1008].  
**Format:** Contract research  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Principal investigator (academic) and Security engineering lead (company)  

### The problem
- The company needs to detect unusual behaviour on Modbus and BACnet networks without labelled attack data, and hand-written rules miss slow, low-volume probing entirely. [C032-CH1]
- Statistical baselining drifts with shift patterns and seasonal production changes, generating noise analysts cannot keep up with. [C032-CH1]
- Any detection model must run inference on the edge sensor, which has 512 MB of RAM and a modest ARM core, and must be deployable within twelve months. [C032-CH1]

### Work packages
1. Can an unsupervised autoencoder trained solely on unlabelled Modbus and BACnet traffic flag anomalous sequences, measured against scripted fault injections on the university testbed?
2. Does an 8-bit quantised and pruned model retain detection performance on BACnet traffic, measured against AUROC on a scripted BACnet fault-injection set created on the university testbed?
3. Does an incremental online model maintain F1 score within an accepted tolerance across a defined concept-drift scenario replayed from the eighteen months of historical logs, measured against a static model retrained on the full log over the same window?
4. Does the deployed model on the EdgeWatch sensor achieve a false-positive rate below a threshold agreed with the company, measured against four weeks of live traffic on one customer site?

### Plan
