# Knowing which transformers will fail before they do

**Company:** GridSense Analytics [C047], Energy software, 31 employees, NTP Novi Sad.  
**Researcher:** Marko Petrović, Full Professor, Faculty of Technical Sciences, Department of Power, Electronics and Telecommunications [R0689].  
**Format:** Joint project  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Principal Investigator (Faculty of Technical Sciences) (academic) and Technical Project Lead (GridSense Analytics) (company)  

### The problem
- Maintenance teams currently rely on age and visual inspection, sometimes replacing transformers that still had years left while missing ones that are quietly deteriorating. [C047-CH1]
- Operators hold dissolved gas analysis results and up to eight years of hourly SCADA load data for roughly four hundred transformers but have no model to turn those into a ranked failure list. [C047-CH1]
- Results are needed within about ten months to feed into the 2027 module release, and the output must be readable by a maintenance planner with no data science background. [C047-CH1]

### Work packages
1. Which combination of DGA gas ratios and SCADA load features best predicts transformer failure within twelve months, measured against documented failure events in the two-operator dataset?
2. Does a Bayesian probabilistic scoring approach produce failure probability ranks that agree with a held-out set of documented failure events withheld from model training?
3. How much of the variance in failure probability scores is explained by load history length, measured across the three-to-eight-year range available in the dataset?
