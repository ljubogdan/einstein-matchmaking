# Ranking transformers by failure risk before the next inspection cycle

**Company:** GridSense Analytics [C047], Energy software, 31 employees, NTP Novi Sad.  
**Researcher:** Marko Petrović, Full Professor, Faculty of Technical Sciences, Department of Power, Electronics and Telecommunications [R0689].  
**Format:** Joint project  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Principal Investigator (academic) and Technical Project Lead (GridSense engineering team) (company)  

### The problem
- Distribution operator customers carry out dissolved gas analysis on their oil-filled transformers every one to two years and have load data from SCADA, but have no way to turn those two sources into a ranked list of transformers most likely to fail. [C047-CH1]
- Maintenance teams currently rely on age and visual inspection, which means they sometimes replace transformers that still had years left and miss ones that are quietly deteriorating. [C047-CH1]
- The output must be readable and actionable by a maintenance planner with no data science background, and must be ready within about ten months to feed into the 2027 module release. [C047-CH1]

### Work packages
1. Which combination of DGA gas features and SCADA-derived load statistics best discriminates transformers that fail within 12 months from those that do not, measured against confirmed failure events in the held-out dataset?
2. Does a gradient-boosted scoring model trained on the two-operator dataset generalise across both operators, measured against per-operator failure recall on the held-out set?
3. Can the risk-score output be rendered as a ranked list with plain-language condition labels that non-specialist planners correctly interpret, measured against planner assessments of a blinded sample drawn from the held-out set and including confirmed failure cases?
