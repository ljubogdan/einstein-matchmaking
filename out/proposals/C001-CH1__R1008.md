# Detecting abnormal Modbus and BACnet traffic without ever seeing an attack

**Company:** ArmaturaSec d.o.o. [C001], Cybersecurity, 67 employees, Zrenjanin.  
**Researcher:** Krisztina Kiss, Full Professor, Faculty of Technical Sciences, Department of Computing and Control Engineering [R1008].  
**Format:** Industrial PhD  
**Supervisors / contacts:** PhD Supervisor, Department of Computing and Control Engineering (academic) and Lead Product Engineer (ProtoSense) (company)  
**Candidate:** a master's graduate in computer engineering, employed by the company for the three years  

### The problem
- The ProtoSense module needs to flag traffic that looks wrong, but no client has ever provided a packet capture containing a confirmed attack. [C001-CH1]
- A supervised classifier trained on public ICS datasets produced so many false positives in real deployments that clients started switching the alerts off. [C001-CH1]
- The company needs a method that learns what normal looks like on a specific site and raises a flag when behaviour drifts from that baseline. [C001-CH1]

### Research questions
1. Can a normal-traffic baseline built from unlabelled Modbus TCP and BACnet/IP captures detect scripted faults injected on the hardware bench in Year 2, measured against technician-labelled fault logs from those scripted fault sessions?
2. Does the baseline model remain stable across sites with different traffic periodicity, measured against per-site false-alarm rates on held-out clean captures from at least three client archives?
3. Can the anomaly scores produced by the model be summarised in a one-page report that plant managers rate as understandable, measured against a structured comprehension test with all available plant managers at the trial site and, if fewer than two are available there, supplemented by plant managers at other consenting ArmaturaSec client sites?
4. Does the method run within the latency budget of the ProtoSense module on the hardware bench, measured against packet-forwarding latency recorded before and after the model is loaded?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 — Baseline pipeline | PhD candidate and research group design feature extraction for Modbus TCP and BACnet/IP payloads and train one-class models on traffic archives provided under NDA by the company. | Baseline model fit and normal-traffic evaluation documented in a technical report; detection performance against fault events is not assessed in this phase. |
