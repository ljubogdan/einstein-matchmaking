# Identifying and Correcting the Oxidation Pathway Limiting Shelf Life of Cold-Pressed Pumpkin Seed Oil

**Company:** Zlatna Njiva d.o.o. [C061], Food production and food tech, 87 employees, Zrenjanin.  
**Faculty:** Faculty of Technology, Department of Applied and Engineering Chemistry [R0231].  
**Format:** Contract research  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Full Professor, Department of Applied and Engineering Chemistry (academic) and Quality/Lab Manager, Zlatna Njiva d.o.o. (company)

### The problem
- Despite dark-glass packaging and nitrogen flushing, peroxide value rises well above the acceptable threshold within the first few months of storage across multiple batches. [C061-CH1]
- Trials with rosemary extract produced no measurable improvement, and the dominant pathway—enzymatic, photo-oxidative, or trace-metal-catalysed—has not been identified. [C061-CH1]
- The company has no gas chromatography equipment and has never performed a fatty acid profile or tocopherol content analysis in-house, preventing root-cause diagnosis. [C061-CH1]

### Work packages
1. Which oxidation pathway—autoxidative, photo-oxidative, or trace-metal-catalysed—is the primary driver of peroxide value rise in this oil?
2. What is the tocopherol composition of the oil across batches, and does it correlate with batch-to-batch variability in oxidative stability?
3. How much residual oxygen remains in the headspace after the current nitrogen-flush procedure, and is that level sufficient to drive the observed deterioration rate?
4. Which combination of modified bottling practice and antioxidant strategy can extend shelf life to commercially competitive levels without major capital expenditure?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 – Baseline Characterisation (months 1–2) | Collect oil samples across multiple batches and bottling dates; determine fatty acid composition by GC-FID and tocopherol content by HPLC-FLD; establish initial peroxide value and p-anisidine value panel. | Milestone 1 report: fatty acid profile and tocopherol baseline across batches, identifying compositional drivers of variability. |
| Phase 2 – Oxidation Kinetics and Bottling Line Assessment (months 3–5) | Conduct accelerated storage trials with full oxidation marker panel including Rancimat induction period; measure residual headspace oxygen directly on the production bottling line; assess photo-oxidative and trace-metal contributions. | Milestone 2 report: oxidation kinetics data and headspace oxygen measurements with ranked dominant pathway findings. |
| Phase 3 – Intervention Testing (months 6–8) | Test modified nitrogen-flush protocols and revised antioxidant combinations against the ranked pathway findings; evaluate feasibility of each intervention within existing infrastructure and without major equipment purchase. | Ranked set of intervention options with supporting stability data, ready for company selection. |
