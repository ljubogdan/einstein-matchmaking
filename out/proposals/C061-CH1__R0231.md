# Finding and fixing the cause of early rancidity in cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil

**Company:** Zlatna Njiva d.o.o. [C061], Food production and food tech, 87 employees, Zrenjanin.  
**Researcher:** Radomir Mirković, Full Professor, Faculty of Technology, Department of Applied and Engineering Chemistry [R0231].  
**Format:** Contract research  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Full Professor, Department of Applied and Engineering Chemistry (academic) and Quality and Production Manager (company)

### The problem
- The oil goes rancid within 4 months on shelf, but competitors manage 9–12 months, and the company loses about 15% of each batch to returns. [C061-CH1]
- The company spends roughly 180,000 RSD per year on returned and written-off stock from this issue alone. [C061-CH1]
- The company does not have gas chromatography equipment and has never done a fatty acid profile breakdown or a tocopherol content analysis in-house. [C061-CH1]

### Work packages
1. Which oxidation pathway — autoxidation, photo-oxidative, enzymatic, or trace-metal mediated — is dominant in the company's cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil?
2. How much residual oxygen remains in the headspace after the current nitrogen-flush protocol?
3. How do the fatty acid profile and tocopherol content of the oil relate to its measured oxidation rate?
4. Which process changes requiring no major capital equipment purchase would most effectively extend shelf life?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline characterisation | Collect fresh and stored oil samples from multiple batches; determine fatty acid profile by GC-FID, tocopherol content by HPLC, and track peroxide value over time to establish baseline oxidation kinetics and link composition to oxidation rate. | Baseline analytical report covering fatty acid profile, tocopherol content, and initial peroxide kinetics, delivered at month two. |
| Headspace oxygen assessment | Measure residual oxygen concentration in bottled product headspace across multiple fills under the current nitrogen-flush protocol and compare results against published thresholds for effective oxidation suppression. | Headspace oxygen measurement report with assessment of current nitrogen-flush effectiveness, delivered at month five. |
| Oxidation pathway identification | Apply Rancimat induction period testing, p-anisidine value measurement, and TBARS analysis to identify primary and secondary oxidation markers; cross-reference with baseline fatty acid and tocopherol data to rank contributing pathways. | Oxidation pathway identification report with ranked contributing factors, delivered at month eight. |
| Process recommendations and quality control | Translate pathway and headspace findings into a ranked set of process changes compatible with existing equipment and develop a quality control protocol for ongoing in-house monitoring. | Final report with implementable process recommendations and quality control protocol, delivered at month twelve. |

- The company runs a dark glass bottling line with nitrogen flushing. [C061]
**University** [R0231]
- The research group's dedicated lipid analysis laboratory holds a rancimat oxidative stability instrument, GC-FID for fatty acid profiling, and HPLC for tocopherol quantification. [R0231]
