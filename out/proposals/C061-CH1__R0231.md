# Extended Shelf Life for Cold-Pressed Pumpkin Seed Oil Through Oxidation Pathway Control

**Company:** Zlatna Njiva d.o.o. [C061], Food production and food tech, 87 employees, Zrenjanin.  
**Faculty:** Faculty of Technology, Department of Applied and Engineering Chemistry [R0231].  
**Format:** Contract research  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Principal Investigator (Full Professor, Applied and Engineering Chemistry) as project lead (academic) and Production/Quality Manager as primary contact and milestone sign-off authority (company)

### The problem
- The oil reaches peroxide values above 15 meq/kg by month four, above the acceptable threshold, while competitors achieve shelf lives of 9–12 months. [C061-CH1]
- The current nitrogen-flush setup uses a single nozzle above the fill point, leaving residual headspace oxygen levels unmeasured and potentially well above safe limits. [C061-CH1]

### Work packages
1. Which oxidation pathway—autoxidation of polyunsaturated fatty acids, photo-sensitised oxidation, or trace-metal catalysis—dominates peroxide accumulation in this oil across the shelf-life cycle?
2. What level of residual headspace oxygen does the current single-nozzle nitrogen flush achieve, and what modified flush protocol can reliably meet a 2% residual oxygen threshold?
3. Does the measured tocopherol content provide sufficient endogenous antioxidant protection, and can a supplementary natural antioxidant strategy deliver meaningful induction-period extension?
4. Which combination of process changes—flush protocol, seed storage conditions, or antioxidant addition—provides the most cost-effective route to a 9–12 month shelf life without major capital investment?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 – Oxidation Pathway Diagnosis (Months 1–3) | Collect oil samples across the shelf-life cycle from multiple production batches; perform Rancimat oxidative stability, GC-FID fatty acid profiling, HPLC-FLD tocopherol quantification, and UV/Vis conjugated diene measurements; correlate results with ERP batch variables. | Identification of the dominant oxidation pathway and a quantified baseline of oil quality parameters, communicated to the company at M3. |
| Phase 2 – Headspace Oxygen and Antioxidant Trials (Months 4–6) | Measure residual headspace oxygen under current flush conditions on the bottling line; test modified flush protocols targeting a reduced residual oxygen level; screen natural antioxidant additions using Rancimat induction period as the primary metric. | Ranked assessment of flush protocol options and antioxidant candidates, with supporting oxidation marker data, completed by M6. |
| Phase 3 – Integrated Process Optimisation (Months 7–9) | Combine the best-performing headspace and antioxidant interventions in a short accelerated-storage validation; apply kinetic modelling to project shelf-life outcomes at ambient storage conditions. | A written report of ranked, implementable process recommendations targeting 9–12 months shelf life, delivered and accepted at M9. |
