# Stopping pumpkin seed oil from going rancid before it reaches the customer

**Company:** Zlatna Njiva d.o.o. [C061], Food production and food tech, 87 employees, Zrenjanin.  
**Researcher:** Radomir Mirković, Full Professor, Faculty of Technology, Department of Applied and Engineering Chemistry [R0231].  
**Format:** Contract research  
**Supervisors / contacts:** Principal investigator responsible for analytical programme and reporting (academic) and Quality/production manager as day-to-day liaison and milestone sign-off (company)  

### The problem
- Cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil hits a peroxide value above the acceptable threshold somewhere between three and five months after bottling. [C061-CH1]
- The company spends roughly 180,000 RSD per year on returned and written-off stock from this issue alone. [C061-CH1]
- The company needs to identify the main oxidation pathway and receive a clear set of process changes implementable without buying major new equipment. [C061-CH1]

### Work packages
1. Which oxidation pathway—autoxidative, photo-oxidative, or trace-metal-catalysed—drives peroxide accumulation in this company's pumpkin seed oil, measured against fatty acid, tocopherol, and secondary marker profiles from twelve production batches?
2. What residual oxygen level remains in the headspace after the current single-nozzle nitrogen flush, measured against the 2% threshold established for small-scale cold-press filling lines?
3. Do adjustments to nitrogen flushing or tocopherol-retaining process steps extend Rancimat induction period to a level consistent with 9–12 months shelf life, measured against Rancimat induction periods of three competitor oils?
4. Which combination of process changes delivers the greatest reduction in peroxide value, measured against peroxide values recorded at month four in a prospective accelerated storage run for each intervention condition?

### Plan
| Phase | Work | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 – Baseline analytical profiling (months 1–3) | University laboratory runs GC-FID fatty acid profiling, HPLC-FLD tocopherol quantification, and UV/Vis plus Rancimat oxidative stability on oil samples from six production batches prepared by the company lab technician. | A dataset of fatty acid composition, tocopherol content, peroxide value, p-anisidine value, and Rancimat induction period for six batches; delivered to the company at M3. |
| Phase 2 – Pathway identification and headspace audit (months 4–6) | University applies kinetic modelling and marker profiling to identify the dominant oxidation pathway. Company procures a calibrated portable headspace oxygen analyser for the on-site audit at its own cost; university specialist conducts the measurement on the bottling line. | A written pathway identification report naming the dominant mechanism and a headspace oxygen audit record, delivered at M6. |
| Phase 3 – Intervention trials (months 7–9) | University designs and runs Rancimat and accelerated storage trials on oil samples treated with adjusted nitrogen flush levels and tocopherol-retaining conditions; company lab technician prepares samples and performs routine titrations. Three competitor oils are purchased for Rancimat benchmarking. | A comparative dataset from intervention trials including peroxide values at month four from the accelerated storage run for each condition, and Rancimat induction periods for three competitor oils, delivered at M9. |
| Phase 4 – Final recommendations (months 10–12) | University interprets all trial data and drafts prioritised process recommendations; company quality manager reviews draft and flags commercially confidential content within 60 days per term sheet. | A final written report listing ranked, equipment-compatible process changes the company can implement on its existing bottling line, delivered at M12. |

### What each side brings
**Company** [C061]
- The company provides oil samples and batch records across production runs, and its lab technician handles sample preparation and routine titration support. [C061]
- The company's dark glass bottling line with nitrogen flushing and its internal peroxide value records are available to the project. [C061]
**University** [R0231]
- The research group operates a dedicated lipid analysis laboratory equipped with GC-FID for fatty acid profiling, HPLC for tocopherol quantification, a Rancimat oxidative stability instrument, and a UV/Vis spectrophotometer array. [R0231]

### Why this pair
- The company does not have gas chromatography equipment and has never done a fatty acid profile breakdown or a tocopherol content analysis in-house, which are the analyses needed to identify the oxidation pathway, and the research group holds exactly those instruments. [C061-CH1] [R0231]
- The research group's prior work on headspace oxygen effects in bottling addresses a challenge the company faces; if the company provides access to its production batches and peroxide records, findings could inform process changes. [SYN-PUB-02921] [C061-CH1]
